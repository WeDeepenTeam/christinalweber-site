About Christina

From hosting dating events in NYC to mapping an entire industry dedicated to love

It started in 2014. Christina was throwing "Underground Unattached" dating events in NYC — convinced that if she could get enough dope people in one room, we'd all live happily ever after.

Two years in, she noticed something the events couldn't overcome. Pressure. Expectations. The nervous energy people walked in with. Craving real answers, she went looking and discovered an entire industry dedicated to the things they never taught us in school. Tools, teachers, frameworks, communication skills, conflict resolution, typologies, tantra. That discovery became WeDeepen — think ClassPass for love and thriving relationships.

Since then, through WeDeepen, Love Club, Love Immersions, The Dating Dojo, Playful Portal, and "Shop & Shag" at Erewhon (featured on the cover of LA Magazine and the Bill Maher Show), Christina has curated top intimacy teachers and conscious experiences for thousands of people, helping them experience the kind of connection they always wanted but didn't know how to have and maintain.

Originally from Annapolis, MD, she's lived in Los Angeles and New York City, and now calls Austin, TX home.

Core Teaching

The Four Pillars of a Conscious Relationship

Of everything I've studied and taught, this is what I found to be most grounded and simple. Four pillars that keep love alive, honest, and transformative — in romance, friendship, business, and beyond.

1

Prioritize Growth

No attachment to outcome. Growth comes first.

2

Own Your Own Sh*t

Take responsibility for your wounds and reactions.

3

All Feelings Are Welcome

No internal process is condemned.

4

Practice Love

In every moment, with every person.

Love is a lifelong practice of shedding ego, armor, and control — combined with ever-increasing acts of sharing. These pillars make that possible.
